Is it in an Opportunity Zone?

Check a ZIP code, a street address, or a census tract number against both Opportunity Zone designation rounds (2018 and 2027) and NMTC low-income tracts. Data: CDFI Fund, Treasury and Census Bureau official lists.

Full sample reports

One-page reports from the command-line tool, which also geocodes exact addresses, checks HUBZone status and reads company websites.

How this page works: a ZIP is matched to its census tracts with the Census Bureau's ZIP Code Tabulation Area relationship files, then each tract is checked against the official lists (2018 round: 8,764 tracts, 2010 boundaries, in effect through Dec 31, 2028; 2027 round: 6,576 tracts, 2020 boundaries, Jan 1, 2027 through Dec 31, 2036; NMTC: 2016-2020 ACS low-income communities). A ZIP can span several tracts, so a ZIP result tells you whether any part of the area is designated; only the exact address decides a specific location.

This is a screening tool. It is not tax, legal, or investment advice, and any Opportunity Zone structure must be confirmed with qualified counsel and against the CDFI Fund's official records before reliance.
