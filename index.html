About the Fund

Closing the Racial Wealth Gap — One Acquisition at a Time

The Jumpstart MAP Fund is a first-of-its-kind initiative combining MAP Lab's deep expertise in minority business acquisition with Jumpstart's proven track record of funding high-potential entrepreneurs.


We believe Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (ETA) is one of the most powerful — and underutilized — pathways to generational wealth for Black Americans. By purchasing and operating established, cash-flowing businesses, our founders skip the early startup risk and step directly into ownership.

Why Acquisition Entrepreneurship

A Proven Path to Ownership

Search funds and ETA offer a distinct — and often superior — route to entrepreneurship, especially for those seeking lower risk and faster value creation.

📈
Day-One Cash Flow
Acquiring an existing business means stepping into operations already generating revenue. No years of runway burn — just real business from day one.
🌟
Lower Risk, Higher Certainty
Established businesses have proven products, existing customers, and operating teams — dramatically de-risking the entrepreneur's path.
⛏
Generational Wealth Creation
Business ownership is the #1 driver of the racial wealth gap. Owning equity that grows, earns, and can be passed down changes everything.
Who We Support

Built for the Next Generation of Black Business Owners

We back Black entrepreneurs at every stage — from early explorers to operators ready to close their first deal.

The Aspiring Searcher
You're drawn to business ownership and intrigued by ETA. We provide education, community, and early-stage capital to get you started.
The Active Searcher
You're actively sourcing deals and moving toward an LOI. We fund your search and surround you with advisors and deal flow tools.
The Acquisition-Ready Operator
You've identified a target and are ready to close. We provide acquisition capital and support your 100-day integration plan.
The Portfolio Builder
You've stabilized one business and want to grow further. We back repeat acquirers with growth capital and operational guidance.
