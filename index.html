About the Fund

Closing the Racial Wealth Gap — One Acquisition at a Time

The Jumpstart MAP Fund is a first-of-its-kind initiative combining MAP Lab's deep expertise in minority business acquisition with Jumpstart's proven track record of funding high-potential entrepreneurs.


We believe Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (ETA) is one of the most powerful — and underutilized — pathways to generational wealth for Black Americans. By purchasing and operating established, cash-flowing businesses, our founders skip the early startup risk and step directly into ownership.

Why Acquisition Entrepreneurship

A Proven Path to Ownership

Search funds and ETA offer a distinct — and often superior — route to entrepreneurship, especially for those seeking lower risk and faster value creation.

📈
Day-One Cash Flow
Acquiring an existing business means stepping into operations that are already generating revenue. No years of runway burn — just real business from day one. That cash flow is the foundation of lasting wealth.
🌟
Lower Risk, Higher Certainty
Established businesses have proven products, existing customers, and operating teams. ETA founders acquire evidence-based opportunities rather than betting on unproven ideas, dramatically de-risking the entrepreneur's path.
⛏
Generational Wealth Creation
Business ownership is the #1 driver of the racial wealth gap. Owning a growing, cash-flowing company builds equity that can be held, sold, or passed down — creating lasting impact for families and communities.
Who We Support

Built for the Next Generation of Black Business Owners

We back Black entrepreneurs at various stages of the acquisition journey — from early explorers to active searchers to operators ready to close their first deal.

The Aspiring Searcher
You're drawn to business ownership and intrigued by ETA but want support understanding the model, building skills, and finding your first opportunity. We provide education, community, and early-stage capital.
The Active Searcher
You're actively sourcing deals, evaluating targets, and moving toward a letter of intent. We fund your search phase and surround you with advisors, deal flow tools, and a network of fellow searchers.
The Acquisition-Ready Operator
You've identified a target and are ready to close. We provide acquisition capital, connect you with debt partners, and support your 100-day integration plan to ensure a strong start as the new owner.
The Portfolio Builder
You've acquired and stabilized one business and have your eye on building a portfolio. We support repeat acquirers with growth capital, operational guidance, and access to our broader ecosystem.
